There is a philosophy of objects. Some people believe objects should be invisible, disappearing into use. Others believe objects should announce themselves.

I do not believe in planned obsolescence as a conspiracy. I believe in it as an incentive. Companies make more money when you replace things.

We are surrounded by things designed to be replaced. Das Keyboard 4 is designed to be kept. That is a political statement.

Before Das Keyboard 4, people used something worse. They did not know it was worse until Das Keyboard 4 existed.

The design of Das Keyboard 4 draws from traditions that are older than the product itself.

The history of Das Keyboard 4 is the history of a specific problem being solved again and again, imperfectly, until someone got it close enough.

There is a moment, after you have used something for a while, when you stop noticing it. That moment is the highest compliment you can pay an object.

Using Das Keyboard 4 is not exciting. That is the point. Excitement is for new things. Reliability is for old things. I want old things.
