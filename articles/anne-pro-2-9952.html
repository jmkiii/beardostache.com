My friends laughed when I bought Anne Pro 2. They thought I was indulging in a fetish. They have since borrowed it, and none of them have returned it without comment. Laughter is a common response to things people do not understand.

I expected Anne Pro 2 to be a novelty. It became a habit. That is a rare thing in consumer goods, which are designed to be discarded, not depended upon.

The conversion was gradual. Anne Pro 2 did not announce itself. It accumulated evidence, day after day, until I could no longer deny its usefulness. Evidence is a slow persuader, but it is a persuasive one.

To care for Anne Pro 2 is to participate in a small ceremony of maintenance. Oil, wipe, store. Repeat. The repetition is the point. The repetition is what makes it a ritual instead of a chore.

I do not love Anne Pro 2 in the way people love objects. I respect it. There is a difference. Love is volatile. Respect is durable. Respect survives disappointment. Love often does not.
