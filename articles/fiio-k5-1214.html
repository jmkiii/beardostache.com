The design decisions behind FiiO K5 were made by people who will never use it. They sit in conference rooms and debate specifications while actual users deal with the consequences.

I took FiiO K5 apart once. Not to fix it, but to see how it was made. The inside was as well-made as the outside. That is rare.

The finish matters. The edges matter. The way the object sits in your hand matters. These are not aesthetic choices in the way the Mac means them.

The seams matter. The edges matter. The way two pieces fit together tells you everything about the care that went into making it.
